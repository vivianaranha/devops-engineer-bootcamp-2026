# Course Roadmap

**Created by School of AI**

## 12-week intensive
- Weeks 1–2: foundations, Linux, Git, builds
- Weeks 3–4: CI/CD, Docker, supply chain
- Weeks 5–6: Kubernetes, Terraform, cloud DevOps
- Weeks 7–8: configuration/secrets, observability, Prometheus/Grafana
- Weeks 9–10: logging, DevSecOps, SRE, platform engineering
- Weeks 11–12: portfolio and capstone

## 24-week pace
One module per week plus project/capstone weeks.
