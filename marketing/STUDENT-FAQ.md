## Do I need Docker/Kubernetes to start?
No. Core concepts and utilities are local-first; those tools are used in later hands-on extensions.

## Is Terraform included?
Yes.

## Is observability included?
Yes—metrics, logs, traces, Prometheus, Grafana, alerting and SLOs.

## Is security included?
Yes—CI permissions, secrets, dependency/image scanning, SBOMs and release gates.
