# Instructor Guide

**Created by School of AI**

Do not grade students on whether they can memorize CLI flags.

Require them to explain:
- what is automated,
- what blocks release,
- how identity is scoped,
- what artifact is deployed,
- what happens when health degrades,
- how rollback works,
- what SLO is affected,
- what the incident teaches the team.
